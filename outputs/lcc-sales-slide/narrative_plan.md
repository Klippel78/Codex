# Narrative Plan: LCC Sales Slide

## Audience
Decision makers reviewing an Aura Light LCC proposal who need a concise reason to choose the new lighting solution.

## Objective
Create one persuasive slide that translates the LCC report into a simple win-win story: lower life-cycle cost and lower environmental impact, without losing the softer benefits of comfort, reliability, and operational calm.

## Narrative Arc
The old installation does not only use electricity; it quietly leaks value. Aura Light's smarter solution turns that hidden leakage into visible savings, reduced climate impact, and a better everyday environment.

## Slide List
1. "Gor gott. Ga plus." A single-slide sales story with three hard proof points, a wallet/planet win-win statement, and one softer value card for people and operations.

## Source Plan
Use the current LCC report values repeatedly referenced in the app summary: 685 573 kr cost saving, 19 757 kWh reduced energy use, and 395 kg CO2-e reduced climate impact. The provided example image is used only as visual inspiration and as a cropped earth-side atmosphere.

## Visual System
Use Aura Light's restrained green/teal palette: #00656C, #007E48, #95C11F, #C6DBCB, #E9EFD0, #97999A. The slide should feel like a polished sales story rather than a technical table.

## Asset Needs
Use the supplied screenshot as a right-side earth visual plate, covered/cropped so no non-editable text is used as slide content. All meaningful text, values, labels, and callouts remain editable PowerPoint objects.

## Editability Plan
Build the slide with editable PowerPoint text boxes, shapes, cards, and speaker notes. The only raster element is the decorative earth visual plate.
